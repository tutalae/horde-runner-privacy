Privacy Policy for Horde Runner

Effective date: 13 October 2026
Developer: 1D Studio ("we", "us")
Contact: happysleepyalpaca@gmail.com

Horde Runner is an idle survival shooter for Android. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (stage, gold, upgrades, settings and purchases already made) is saved on your device. We do not receive it unless a feature below says so. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, local progress can be lost.

Signing in is optional. Every ad is optional: ads play only after you tap a button to watch one.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising outside the app.
  • The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party services

  • Google Play services, Play Games Services and Play Billing: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase Authentication: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Your choices

  • Ads: in Android Settings > Google > Ads you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization.
  • Notifications: turn them off in MENU > REMINDERS or in Android settings.
  • Account and data deletion: sign out in MENU > ACCOUNT. To have your account data deleted, email us at the address above and we will delete it within 30 days. Deleting an account does not refund purchases.
  • Local progress: clear the app's data in Android settings.

Delete your account and data (Horde Runner)

To delete your Horde Runner account and its data:

  1. Email happysleepyalpaca@gmail.com from the email address of the Google account you signed in with. Use the subject "Delete my Horde Runner account".
  2. We delete your account record (name, email address, user ID) and any server-side purchase records within 30 days and confirm by email.
  3. Game progress stored on your device is not on our servers: clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Kept: payment records that tax or accounting law requires us to keep. Google Play keeps its own purchase history under your Google account. Deleting an account does not refund purchases.

Retention and security

Account and purchase records are kept while your account exists and as long as the law requires for payment records. Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above. Material changes will be shown in the app or on the store page.
